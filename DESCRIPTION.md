# Voxy World Gen V2 - NeoForge Port

**Minecraft 1.20.1 / 1.21.1 - NeoForge / Forge**

A custom NeoForge port of [Voxy World Gen V2](https://modrinth.com/mod/voxy-worldgen) - the background chunk pre-generation companion mod for Voxy. Required for the best Voxy LoD experience.

---

## What does it do?

Voxy needs chunks to be generated before it can build LoD data for them. Without pre-generation, you will see blank LoD tiles in unvisited areas. Voxy World Gen V2 solves this by silently pre-generating chunks in the background while you play, so your LoD map fills in automatically without any manual effort.

- Background generation - no commands needed, runs automatically
- Prioritises areas near the player first
- Configurable radius and generation speed
- Works alongside Voxy's LoD rendering seamlessly
